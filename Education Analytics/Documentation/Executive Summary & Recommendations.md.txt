# Executive Summary & Recommendations

> **Notice on Data Authenticity:** *This report synthesizes performance and attendance data across Basic 6, JHS1, JHS2, and JHS3 cohorts for Mathematics and Science at Glamersco Citadel Educational Complex. All metrics were analyzed using 100% synthetic dataset records created for training, SQL verification, and portfolio validation. School references and administrative contexts serve strictly as a realistic analytical scenario and do not represent confidential or real-world student records.*

## Key Findings

1. **Class-Level Rankings:**
   - **Basic 6** demonstrated the highest academic performance across both subjects (Math: 70.23%, Science: 74.03%).
   - Performance steadily declined across higher grade levels: JHS1 (70.22% / 73.06%), JHS2 (68.47% / 71.27%), and JHS3 (66.33% / 68.67%).

2. **Subject Disparity:**
   - Science performance consistently exceeded Mathematics performance across every cohort.

3. **Attendance & Academic Risk Association:**
   - Within this synthetic sample, an observed positive association exists between attendance rates and academic standing.
   - All 9 unique students (18 subject records) with attendance under 70% fell directly into the "Needs Support" performance band (<60% average score).

## Strategic & Policy Recommendations

1. **Proactive Early Warning Attendance Tracking:**
   - **Data-Derived Evidence:** 100% of students in this sample with attendance below 70% required academic support (<60% average score).
   - **Policy Recommendation:** Implement an automated administrative alert system when student attendance falls below an **80% early-warning threshold**. This serves as a conservative policy trigger to enable proactive parental engagement before attendance and scores drop below passing thresholds.

2. **Targeted Mathematics Intervention:**
   - Allocate supplementary instructional hours and peer-tutoring sessions for JHS2 and JHS3 Mathematics cohorts to stem upper-grade score regression prior to national final examinations.

## Analytical Limitations
- **Synthetic Sample:** Results are based on a 72-row synthetic dataset designed for portfolio and query verification.
- **Scope:** Restricted to two core subjects over a single academic term using unweighted assessment averages.
