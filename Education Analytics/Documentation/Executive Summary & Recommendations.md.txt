# Executive Summary & Recommendations

## Overview
This report synthesizes performance and attendance data across Basic 6, JHS1, JHS2, and JHS3 cohorts for Mathematics and Science at Glamersco Citadel Educational Complex.

> **Data Integrity Notice:** All metrics were analyzed using synthetic dataset records created for training and portfolio validation.

## Key Findings

1. **Class-Level Rankings:**
   - **Basic 6** demonstrated the highest academic performance across both subjects (Math: 70.23%, Science: 74.03%).
   - Performance steadily declined across higher grade levels: JHS1 (70.22% / 73.06%), JHS2 (68.47% / 71.27%), and JHS3 (66.33% / 68.67%).

2. **Subject Disparity:**
   - Science performance consistently exceeded Mathematics performance across every cohort.

3. **Attendance & Academic Risk Association:**
   - A clear relationship exists between attendance rates and academic standing.
   - All 9 unique students (18 subject records) with attendance under 70% fell directly into the "Needs Support" performance band (<60% average score).

## Actionable Recommendations

1. **Early Warning Attendance Tracking:**
   - Implement an automated administrative alert system when student attendance falls below 80% to enable proactive parental engagement before scores drop below passing thresholds.

2. **Targeted Mathematics Intervention:**
   - Allocate supplementary instructional hours and peer-tutoring sessions for JHS2 and JHS3 Mathematics cohorts to stem upper-grade score regression.
